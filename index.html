An Excel-based healthcare analysis of 400 patient records, examining recovery outcomes, treatment effectiveness, departmental performance, treatment costs and patient demographics.

Global HIV Burden & Treatment Coverage Analysis

A Power BI public health analysis examining global HIV trends from 2000 to 2024, with a focus on new infections, HIV-related deaths, people living with HIV, treatment coverage and differences in disease burden across countries.

The analysis uses a star-schema data model and an interactive dashboard to explore how key HIV indicators have changed over time and where disparities in burden and treatment coverage remain.

Key Findings:
• In 2024, 35.23M people were living with HIV globally.
• New infections were 984K, while HIV-related deaths were 523K in 2024.
• From 2000 to 2024, new infections declined by 59.02%, while HIV-related deaths declined by 65.50%.
• South Africa recorded the highest HIV burden in the 2024 analysis.
• Treatment coverage remained uneven across countries, including among countries with high infection levels.

Tools:
Power BI • Power Query • DAX • Data Modelling

VitalCare Performance Analysis

A Power BI healthcare analysis comparing 2023 and 2024 performance across operational, clinical and financial outcomes.

The analysis examines changes in patient demand, clinical outcomes, resource utilisation and financial performance, with dedicated views covering admissions, readmissions, length of stay, bed occupancy, mortality, revenue, costs, profit and departmental performance.

Key Findings:
• Admissions increased by 4.08%, while total patients increased by 3.43%.
• Revenue increased by 5.75%, while profit increased by 4.70%.
• Mortality rate decreased by 15.77%.
• Readmission rate increased by 8.68%, highlighting an area for further investigation around discharge planning and patient follow-up.
• Profit margin declined slightly from 14.82% to 14.67% as costs increased faster than revenue.
• General Surgery recorded the highest admissions, while Oncology was the strongest contributor to revenue and profit.

Workforce Attrition Analysis

A Tableau and Excel workforce analytics project analysing employee attrition, demographics, tenure, satisfaction and departmental performance across 1,485 employees.

The analysis identified workforce segments with elevated attrition risk and moved beyond simple employee exit counts by comparing proportional attrition rates across departments, age groups, education fields and tenure.

1,485 Employees Analysed
243 Employees Left
16.4% Overall Attrition

Key Insights

  • Sales recorded the highest departmental attrition rate at 20.54%.
  • Employees aged 18–26 showed the highest age-group attrition rate at 34.57%.
  • Employees within their first two years at the company recorded an attrition rate of 30.43%.
  • The analysis demonstrated why attrition rates should be considered alongside absolute employee exit counts when identifying workforce risk.
